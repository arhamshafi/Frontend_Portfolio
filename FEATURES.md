## 🎨 Design System

### Colors
- **Primary**: Purple (#8b5cf6)
- **Background**: Black (#000000)
- **Text**: White (#ffffff)
- **Secondary Text**: Gray (#9ca3af)
- **Borders**: Dark Purple (#1f1f1f)

### Typography
- **Font**: Inter (Google Fonts)
- **Headings**: Bold, 2xl-5xl
- **Body**: Regular, base-lg
- **Labels**: Medium, sm-base

### Spacing
- **Section Padding**: py-20
- **Container**: max-w-6xl / max-w-7xl
- **Grid Gap**: gap-6 / gap-8
- **Card Padding**: p-6

### Animations
- **Duration**: 0.3s - 0.8s
- **Easing**: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)
- **Hover**: scale(1.05), translateY(-5px)
- **Scroll**: fade in, slide in
